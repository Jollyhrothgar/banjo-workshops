          About this recording

          On October 17, 2006, banjo innovator Bill Keith (1939–2015) taught a three-hour online workshop for members of the Banjo Lounge, a banjo community that met in an iVocalize voice-chat room. Bill talked and played over the room's live audio, put handwritten tab on a shared whiteboard, and answered questions from the text chat.

          The session covered right-hand roll patterns (forward, backward, reverse and Foggy Mountain rolls), thumb, middle and index leads, major and pentatonic scales, the modes of the G scale and the chords built on them, the D7 tritone, and the circle of fifths.

          The room's software saved the session as a Java-applet replay, which modern browsers can no longer run. This page rebuilds it from the original recording file: the audio, all 14 slide changes, the whiteboard drawings and the 397 chat messages, each at the moment it happened.

          The recorder only captured audio while someone was speaking, so silences, including the intermission, are shortened. Chat and slides stay in sync with the audio throughout.
